Chat list header — four directions

Replacing the dual-label "CHATS · BY CLIENT · RECENT" arrangement. Sidebar width and row styling match production.

1 · Drop the "Chats" label

Toggle is the header. Two labels competing becomes one. Active = darker + heavier; inactive = muted. No separator.
