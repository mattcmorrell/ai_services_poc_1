Design Notes
Chat List Sort
Three sort modes: Recent (default today), Next Meeting (sorted by soonest calendar event linked to client), Deadline (sorted by soonest compliance/task deadline). Meeting badges are always visible regardless of sort mode - sort just changes the order.
Meeting Badges
Three urgency tiers: Red = within 3 hours, Blue = within 48 hours, Green = this week. Deadline badges (amber) are separate and can appear alongside meeting badges. Badges show on chat items regardless of view or sort.
Proactive Agent Banner
Appears on dashboard when a meeting is < 3 hours away. Links to the relevant agent with a one-click action. Dismissible. Only one banner at a time (most urgent meeting wins).
Calendar as Source of Truth
Pandopticon reads from the calendar and reacts. "Schedule Meeting" action plan steps create events on approval (writes to calendar). The meeting context bar in chat view pulls from calendar data to show upcoming meetings for the active client. No calendar UI needed in Pandopticon itself - it's a smart overlay.
